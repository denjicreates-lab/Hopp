Hopp is a free, open-source music player app that combines Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and JioSaavn playlists in one place. Features include a unified music library, fully customizable themes and navigation, AI mood-based song recommendations, song recognition (identify songs by listening), local music player with FLAC and WAV support, and lossless downloads from open-licensed music sources. Hopp is a customizable music player with an Apple Music inspired design, built for music lovers who want one app for all their playlists. Supports Hindi, English and Hinglish. Requires the respective streaming subscriptions for full playback on each platform.
